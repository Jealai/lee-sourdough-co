Our Story

Bread made the way it used to be made.

Lee Sourdough Co. is a small home bakery owned and operated by Savannah Lee. Every loaf is mixed, shaped, and baked by hand — no shortcuts, no commercial additives, just time and care.

How It Started

A starter, a kitchen, and a lot of patience.

Savannah started baking sourdough the way most people do — a jar of flour and water on the counter, and a lot of trial and error. What began as a hobby turned into something neighbors started asking for by name. Lee Sourdough Co. grew out of that: a one-woman, home-based bakery built loaf by loaf.

There's no factory, no mass production, and no rushing the process. Just a home baker who believes good bread takes time — and that the slower rhythms of homemade baking are worth protecting.

What We Believe

Simple ingredients. Slow process. No shortcuts.

Time, Not Shortcuts

Long, slow fermentation instead of commercial yeast — it's what gives each loaf real flavor and a crackling crust.

Just a Few Ingredients

Flour, water, salt, and a natural starter. That's it — nothing artificial, nothing you can't pronounce.

Made by Hand, By One Person

Every loaf that leaves this kitchen has been shaped and scored by Savannah herself — small batches, always.

The Fine Print

Who's actually behind Lee Sourdough Co.

Lee Sourdough Co. is a trade name used by Savannah Lee, who operates this bakery as a sole proprietor. There's no corporation or franchise here — just one person, baking bread, running the business herself. If you ever need to reach the business directly, you're reaching Savannah.
