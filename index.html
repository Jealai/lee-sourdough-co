Baked by hand, one loaf at a time.

Savannah Lee is a home baker who believes good bread shouldn't be rushed. No shortcuts, no additives, just flour, water, salt, and a starter that's been fed and cared for like family.

How It's Made

From starter to your table.

Feed the Starter

A natural levain, fed daily, gives every loaf its flavor and rise — no commercial yeast needed.

Mix & Rest

Simple ingredients come together, then rest through a slow, cool fermentation for real flavor.

Shape & Score

Each loaf is shaped by hand and scored with its own pattern before it goes in the oven.

Bake & Deliver

Baked fresh and brought straight to you — still warm, always local.
